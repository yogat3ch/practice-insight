# Exporting Your Insight Timer Data

How to export your meditation history from the Insight Timer app as a CSV for use with Practice Insight.

<!-- app-it-export:start -->

## Export from the app

| Step | Image |
|------|-------|
| 1. From the mobile app home screen, click the three lines in the top left. | ![Three Lines Top Left](static/it_export/three_lines.webp) |
| 2. Click the cog/gear icon at the bottom labeled Settings. | ![Settings Cog](static/it_export/settings.webp) |
| 3. Open the 'Features and Preferences' section. | ![Features and Preferences](static/it_export/features_preferences.webp) |
| 4. Click Sessions. | ![Sessions](static/it_export/sessions.webp) |
| 5. Click the last option: Export log. | ![Export Log](static/it_export/export_log.webp) |

> **Tip:** The exported file uses the columns `Started At`, `Duration`, `Preset`, `Activity` — exactly what Practice Insight expects.

## Bring it into Practice Insight

1. Open Practice Insight.
2. Drag-and-drop your CSV onto the app (or click to browse).
3. Check the parse count badge to confirm your sessions loaded.
