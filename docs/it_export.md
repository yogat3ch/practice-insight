# Exporting Your Insight Timer Data

How to export your meditation history from the Insight Timer app as a CSV for use with Practice Insight.

<!-- app-it-export:start -->

## Export from the app

1. Open the Insight Timer app and go to your profile.
2. Tap the menu and choose **Export Data**.
3. Select the CSV format and save the file to your device.

> **Tip:** The exported file uses the columns `Started At`, `Duration`, `Preset`, `Activity` — exactly what Practice Insight expects.

## Bring it into Practice Insight

1. Open Practice Insight.
2. Drag-and-drop your CSV onto the app (or click to browse).
3. Check the parse count badge to confirm your sessions loaded.
